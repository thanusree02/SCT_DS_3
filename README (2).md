# Customer Purchase Analysis Dashboard – Bank Marketing Dataset

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer purchase behavior using the Bank Marketing Dataset. The dashboard was built in Power BI to identify customer segments, purchase patterns, and marketing opportunities.

## Tools Used

* Power BI
* DAX
* Bank Marketing Dataset

## Dashboard Features

### KPI Cards

* Total Customers
* Purchased Customers
* Not Purchased Customers
* Purchase Rate %

### Visualizations

* Purchase Distribution
* Customers by Job
* Customers by Education
* Marital Status Analysis
* Average Balance by Job

### Filters

* Job
* Education
* Marital Status
* Housing Loan
* Personal Loan

## Key Insights

* Most customers did not subscribe to the term deposit.
* Customers with higher account balances show better purchase potential.
* Purchase behavior varies across different job and education groups.
* Targeted marketing can improve conversion rates.
